# Portfolio presentation

## Resume bullet

Built ShipSearch, a seven-page responsive Dubai courier portfolio website with three service landing pages, a browser-only enquiry brief builder, technical SEO validation and a reproducible analysis of 10,999 Kaggle shipping records; documented a 12-keyword intent map, three-competitor comparison and controlled before/after audit.

## A three-minute walkthrough

1. Open the homepage. Explain the individual / merchant audience and the preparation-first content.
2. Open international shipping. Point to its unique title, heading, customs questions and internal service link.
3. Build an enquiry using a 2 kg, 30 × 20 × 15 cm parcel. Show the 1.8 kg volumetric comparison and the 2 kg illustrative chargeable result. Download the brief. Explain why no real price is fabricated.
4. Open the case study. Show data provenance and the direction of the dataset label. Explain why it cannot establish UAE delivery performance.
5. Show the keyword map, official competitor sources and audit fixture. Run `npm run build` and `npm test`.

## Be ready to discuss

- Static HTML exposes meaningful content without a JavaScript runtime; routes are actual folders rather than a client-side fallback.
- Small, dependency-free modules keep the frontend portable and easy to inspect. This is intentionally not an oversized application framework.
- No real backend or courier integration is implemented. The enquiry tool provides a useful local artifact and states that boundary explicitly.
- SEO implementation is not a claim of ranking improvement. No Search Console or traffic history exists.
- AI assisted the implementation. Present the design decisions, code and research you have reviewed and can explain; do not claim measured results or independent work you did not perform.
